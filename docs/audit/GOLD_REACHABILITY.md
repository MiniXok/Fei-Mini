# Audit 1: Gold Program Reachability & Grammar Representability

**Audit Date:** 2026-11-08  
**Scope:** Development Set (100 evaluated tasks, `datasets/dev/dev_0000.json` to `dev_0099.json`)  
**Investigator:** Independent Scientific Reviewer / Fei-Mini Engineering  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Scientific Finding

A central requirement of scientific benchmarking in program synthesis is:
> **"Do not call a search space combinatorially difficult when the intended solution is not representable by the search grammar."**

Our audit revealed a critical discrepancy between the program generator (`src/feimini/generator/programs.py`) and the exhaustive candidate enumerator (`src/feimini/search/exhaustive.py`):
- **100% (100/100)** of gold programs are statically well-typed and execute deterministically across all visible and hidden database instances.
- However, **only 27% (27/100)** of gold programs are structurally representable and reachable within 2,500 candidates by the current exhaustive enumerator.
- **73% (73/100)** of gold programs could **never** be generated by the enumerator, regardless of candidate budget, due to vocabulary and grammar mismatches.
- Semantically equivalent programs were discovered for **33% (33/100)** of tasks at budget 2048 (the 27 structurally identical plus 6 semantically equivalent alternatives).

Therefore, part of the low solve rate reported in M4 was caused by **grammar and vocabulary truncation**, not purely combinatorial search difficulty.

## 3. Discrepancy Taxonomy: Why Were 73 Gold Programs Unreachable?

By comparing each unreachable gold AST against the candidate generation rules in `ExhaustiveSearchSolver._generate_candidates`, we identified four primary root causes:

### A. Column Alias Discrepancy in Aggregation (`as_col`) (23 tasks)
- **Generator:** `ProgramGenerator` emitted `CountNode(..., as_col="total_cnt")`, `SumNode(..., as_col="sum_metric")`, and `SumNode(..., as_col="agg_metric")`.
- **Enumerator:** `ExhaustiveSearchSolver` hardcoded `CountNode(..., as_col="cnt")` and `SumNode(..., as_col="sum_val")`.
- **Verifier Impact:** `compare_tables` in `src/feimini/verifier/comparison.py` strictly checks column names (`col_act.name != col_exp.name`). Because the alias names never matched, all 23 aggregation tasks were rejected as `OUTPUT_SCHEMA_MISMATCH`.

### B. Omission of `limit=4` in Enumerator (12 tasks)
- **Generator:** `ProgramGenerator` sampled `limit = rng.randint(2, 5)`, which generates `limit in {2, 3, 4, 5}`.
- **Enumerator:** `ExhaustiveSearchSolver` iterated over `for lim in [2, 3, 5]:`, omitting `limit=4`.
- **Impact:** Any task with a gold AST containing `limit=4` was structurally unrepresentable.

### C. Omission of `NEQ` Operator on Numerical Columns (4 tasks)
- **Generator:** `ProgramGenerator` used `PredicateOp.NEQ` on categorical integer columns (e.g. `credits != 4`).
- **Enumerator:** `ExhaustiveSearchSolver` restricted numerical columns to `[EQ, GT, LT]`, omitting `NEQ`.

### D. Depth & Constant Range Truncation (39 tasks)
- **Continuous Float Literals:** `ProgramGenerator` sampled random floats (`round(rng.uniform(50.0, 250.0), 2)`) that did not exist in any database row (e.g., `weight_kg > 157.1` in `dev_0001`). The enumerator only searched over constants extracted directly from database rows (`constants_by_col`).
- **Depth-4 Compositions:** Deeper compositions (e.g. `Scan -> Filter -> Sort -> Limit`) were excluded from the enumerator's bounded depth loops.
- **Projection Width Cap:** In `ExhaustiveSearchSolver`, projections were bounded by `for k in range(1, min(len(col_names) + 1, 3)):`, which in Python evaluates to `k in {1, 2}`. 3-column projections generated by `ProgramGenerator` (`k in {1, 2, 3}`) were omitted.

## 4. Scientific Implications for Experiment 001

1. **Validity of the Baseline Numbers:**
   - The reported solve rate of 33% at budget 2048 was effectively solving **33 out of the ~35 tasks that were representable by the enumerator**.
   - Within the reachable sub-grammar, exhaustive enumeration is actually quite effective for shallow programs, but fails for depth >= 3.
2. **Requirement for Milestone 5:**
   - When training a learned policy, the action vocabulary of the learned controller and the baseline enumerators must be aligned so that both operate on an identical, well-defined grammar.
   - Continuous literal constants should either be discretized or sampled strictly from active database active domains.
